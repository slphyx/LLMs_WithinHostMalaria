# Literature research

- Search for the precise biological or modelling claim, not a broad topic.
- Prefer original studies, methods papers, official documentation, and stable DOI or journal pages.
- Record title, authors, year, DOI or URL, and the specific claim each source supports.
- Verify claims against the source text; do not infer support from a search snippet alone.
- Separate evidence from interpretation and note when evidence is indirect or model-dependent.
